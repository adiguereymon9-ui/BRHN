## Good to know

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or call us at least 2 hours before your time.

Is there a minimum spend for laptop tables?

No minimum. We ask that you order something every couple of hours so the space stays fair for everyone.

Do you do oat, almond or lactose-free milk?

All three, at no extra charge. Tell us about allergies in the booking notes.

Can I buy beans online?

Not yet. Pick up a bag in the shop or reserve one in your booking notes.
